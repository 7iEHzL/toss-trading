# Paper-discovery Bridge

Alpha Lab discovers empirical phenomena. PQD attempts to kill proposed publication questions.

A `PAPER_OBSERVATION` may be logged for structured regime instability, surprising cross-sectional
heterogeneity, repeated mechanism failure, inference-changing implementation choices, economically
ordered decay, or a credible literature discrepancy. It is not evidence of novelty or a paper.

Required fields:

- `observation_id`
- `source_alpha_ids`
- `empirical_pattern`
- `why_surprising`
- `possible_economic_mechanism`
- `closest_literature`
- `alternative_explanations`
- `data_mining_risk`
- `replication_needed`
- `PQD_status`

Only observations replicated under the relevant alpha governance and with an economic question enter
PQD. PQD literature search, novelty audit, identification and referee attack remain independent filters.
