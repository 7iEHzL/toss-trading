# Alpha Lab v1 Design Report

## Primary objective

Systematically discover, falsify and validate quantitative alpha signals without turning repeated tests
into an undocumented Sharpe search.

## Secondary publication objective

Preserve replicated, economically structured findings as Paper Observations. Only PQD can promote them
to publication questions after novelty and identification attacks.

## WorldQuant model findings

Official public material verifies predictive alpha expressions, historical data/predefined operators,
simulated equity weights, OOS qualification, relative scoring, quality/quantity and a correlation test.
Public formulas and thresholds for Sharpe, Fitness, decay, truncation and submission tests were not
verified and remain `UNKNOWN`. Local equivalents are not represented as WorldQuant replicas.

## Recommended system

- Research unit/ID: immutable `Axxx`; variants `Axxx-vNN` preserve lineage.
- Primary metrics: Pearson IC, rank IC, IC mean/dispersion/IR, positive-IC frequency and quantile
  monotonicity/spread.
- Secondary metrics: decay, coverage, persistence, turnover, costs, concentration, exposure and
  redundancy.
- Multiple testing: complete ledger; cohort/family test counts; FDR only for valid comparable families;
  DSR/PBO only in later portfolio/search settings.
- Parameters: classify economic, implementation and optimization parameters; freeze finite variant budgets
  before results.

## Universe and time

Best free-data direction is a frozen current broad liquid-equity roster for explicitly survivorship-limited
discovery plus a prospective membership/exit ledger for future clean shadow validation. A hand-stitched
historical free universe is rejected after R3/R4B. Final universe criteria require a separate data-only audit.

2007–2022 remains contaminated prior research history. Final OOS 2023–2025 remains `SEALED` and outside
Alpha Lab. A future prospective cohort is the first clean validation period.

## Main risks

- Statistical: hidden multiple testing and dependent observations.
- Data: survivorship, delistings and missing PIT fundamentals/events in free sources.
- Process: converting discovery into optimization through unrecorded neighboring variants.

Do not yet build a formula generator, optimizer, ML search, live integration, portfolio combiner or
WorldQuant submission automation.
