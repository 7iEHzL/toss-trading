## Recommendation

Use a **dual track**, subject to a later data-only freeze:

1. A frozen current broad liquid-equity roster for survivorship-limited historical discovery.
2. The same roster plus a prospective membership/identifier/delisting ledger for genuinely clean future
   shadow validation.

The nine R4 ETFs can calibrate mechanics but are too few for primary cross-sectional IC research. Do not
attempt another hand-stitched historical universe. Final eligibility, liquidity floor, exchange/security
types and freeze timestamp remain undecided until a separate universe audit; no alpha results may inform
them.
