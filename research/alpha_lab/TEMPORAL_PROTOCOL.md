# Alpha Lab Temporal Protocol

## Contamination map

- 2007–2014: directly observed in R5 and not clean.
- 2015–2022: repeatedly observed in R1–R5 and not clean.
- 2023–2025: legacy Final OOS; remains sealed and excluded from Alpha Lab.
- From the Alpha Lab protocol/universe freeze forward: eligible for prospective shadow evidence only.

Changing strategy families does not clean previously inspected dates.

## Proposed phases

1. **Historical discovery:** a later preregistration chooses a fixed pre-2023 interval. All results are
   labeled `RESEARCHER_CONTAMINATED_DISCOVERY`.
2. **Historical robustness:** non-overlapping subperiod and alternate-universe diagnostics, also
   contaminated; no clean-OOS language.
3. **Prospective shadow:** predictions and data are timestamped before outcomes arrive; no parameter
   changes within a cohort. This becomes Alpha Lab's first genuinely clean validation evidence.

The 2023–2025 seal is not consumed to accelerate Alpha Lab. A future request to open it remains subject
to the existing double-confirmation rule.

## Cohorts

Alphas preregistered together form a cohort with a fixed hypothesis count, dataset version and decision
rules. Later variants enter a new cohort and cannot revise an earlier cohort's conclusions.
