# Alpha Families

This taxonomy organizes hypotheses; it does not authorize formula proliferation.

| Family | Subfamilies | Primary risk |
|---|---|---|
| PRICE | momentum, reversal, breakout, trend, gap, distance from high | overlapping formulas and timing leakage |
| VOLUME_LIQUIDITY | abnormal volume, turnover, dollar volume, liquidity change, Amihud-style measures | stale/zero volume and microcap domination |
| VOLATILITY | realized/downside volatility, change, asymmetry, idiosyncratic volatility | mechanical low-risk exposure |
| FUNDAMENTAL | value, profitability, quality, investment, growth | filing availability and PIT lag |
| EVENT | earnings, analyst revisions, corporate actions | announcement timestamps and selection bias |
| CROSS_SECTIONAL | relative strength, ranks, industry-relative transforms | universe and neutralization dependence |
| INTERACTION | momentum×volatility, value×quality, signal×liquidity | hidden parameter search and sparse support |

Each card has one primary family and may list secondary tags. Family-level experiment counts are part of
the multiple-testing budget.
