# Q06 Citation and Author-cluster Search

## Seeds and Paths

- Ghysels, Horan & Moench → FRBNY versions, citations, Wan and Huang real-time bond papers.
- Real-time bond cluster → nonlinear/weak-factor and Bayesian-learning papers.
- Factor-timing cluster → *Factor Timing*, *Time-Varying Factor Allocation*, macro-conditioned
  factor allocation and conditional factor conference work.
- Revision cluster → Akey, Robertson & Simutin's factor-vintage paper and CRSP revision research.
- Anomaly-macro cluster → He, Su & Yu's subjective expectation revisions and initial-release controls.
- Volatility cluster → Moreira–Muir, Cederburg et al., DeMiguel et al., real-time managed portfolios.
