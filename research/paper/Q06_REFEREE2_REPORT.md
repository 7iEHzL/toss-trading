# Referee #2 Report — Q06

Recommendation: `REJECT IN CURRENT FORM`.

The paper applies a known real-time-data critique to factor returns. Ghysels, Horan and Moench, Wan
et al., and Huang et al. already establish that return-predictability conclusions depend on the macro
information vintage. Vincenz and Zeissler already use vintage-aware macro predictors to time a broad
cross-asset factor universe. He, Su and Yu directly link macro expectation revisions to heterogeneous
anomaly returns. The paper that most makes this contribution look incremental is *Time-Varying Factor
Allocation*, supplemented by *Forecasting through the Rear-View Mirror*.

The purported vintage-only identification is also incomplete. Akey, Robertson and Simutin show that
French factor histories themselves change across download vintages because of methodology and raw-data
updates. Holding today's factor series fixed estimates a researcher's hindsight classification effect,
not an investable real-time factor-timing difference.

The regime indicator invites specification search, macro releases have ragged publication lags, and
factor families create multiple testing. Adding volatility-managed returns is a second crowded paper,
not a solution. Unless the authors state an economic implication beyond “coefficients change when data
change,” Q06 is an obvious extension rather than a publication contribution.
