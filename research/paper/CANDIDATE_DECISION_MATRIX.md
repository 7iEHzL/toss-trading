## Binding Constraints

- C1: novelty and unresolved mechanism; recent papers are very close.
- C2: endogenous event/path definitions and few independent crises.
- C3: matched futures/ETF data, roll/financing comparability.
- C4: essentially scooped by Kurth et al. (2026) and demanding microstructure data.

No candidate is authorized. C2 is the most feasible alternative for undergraduate work if its event
definition is externally fixed; C3 has the cleanest comparison but difficult data.
