# PQD-001 Top-five Referee Tournament

## Q03 Drawdown Geometry

- Referee: path measures are endogenous transformations of returns; few crises and overlapping events
  make inference weak. Momentum-crash literature already covers rebounds.
- Rebuttal: freeze event dates externally and define path variables without strategy returns.
- Verdict: survives narrowly; mechanism remains conditional.

## Q06 Macro Vintage and Factor State Dependence

- Referee: this may merely repeat real-time bond-predictability papers on factor returns.
- Rebuttal: the estimand is paired measurement distortion in conditional factor/scaling coefficients,
  not a new forecast. Same observations and models isolate vintage choice.
- Verdict: survives. A null directly shows whether ex-post state labels matter.

## Q11 Rebalance Timing Luck

- Referee: practitioner literature already knows timing luck; calendar offsets are mechanical.
- Rebuttal: contribution must be inference/measurement—treat all offsets as one randomized strategy
  family and test whether published conclusions survive family-level uncertainty.
- Verdict: survives at Tier C; field-journal upside limited.

## Q12 ETF versus Futures

- Referee: instrument differences are obvious and matched data are expensive; attribution is accounting.
- Rebuttal: synchronized pairs could distinguish roll, financing, short and tracking components.
- Verdict: rebuttal depends on unavailable data; eliminate.

## Q16 Real-time versus NBER Crisis Labels

- Referee: NBER dates are retrospective by design and investors do not literally trade the label.
- Rebuttal: the question is interpretation bias in academic crisis-alpha claims, not tradability.
- Verdict: informative but nested inside Q06; merge rather than retain.
