# Q06 Literature Map

## Semantic Duplication

- Rear-View Mirror / Wan: factor returns would be an adjacent dependent variable. A Q06 table would
  be a substantial extension, but the central hindsight-bias conclusion is already known.
- Time-Varying Factor Allocation: an explicit revised-vintage comparison would be a natural and
  important robustness section; likely `MINOR TO SUBSTANTIAL EXTENSION`, not clearly a new paper.
- Business Cycles and Currency Returns: replacing currencies with factors alone would be a
  `MINOR EXTENSION`.
- Noisy Factors: does not answer macro-vintage Q06, but forces a two-sided vintage design or a
  defensible fixed-outcome interpretation.

## Obvious-extension Test

`BORDERLINE`: A is known—revised macro data alter return-predictability inference. B is known—macro
states and real-time-aware predictors condition factor returns. Combining A and B is not automatically
novel. Q06 would need to establish that vintage revision creates a distinctive cross-factor pattern or
changes an economic interpretation, not merely that coefficients differ.

## Volatility-managed Subquestion

Diagnosis: `SEPARATE QUESTION / UNNECESSARILY BROAD`. Volatility management has its own mature
literature, and adding it increases outcomes, models and multiple testing without strengthening the
core macro-vintage estimand. This diagnosis does not redesign Q06 during the audit.
