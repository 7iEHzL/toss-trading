## Frozen Dimensions

1. Information: real-time versus latest/revised macro vintage.
2. Outcome: factor conditional premia/state dependence and volatility-managed factor conclusions.
3. Contrast: hold returns, dates, portfolios and model fixed; change only macro vintage.
4. Estimand: paired change in conditional means/coefficients/inference attributable to macro vintage.
5. Economics: can hindsight in revised macro data create conditional factor conclusions?

Levels 4–5 are fatal. A Level 3 plus an obvious dependent-variable substitution triggers YELLOW
or RED. No question, variable, regime or outcome was modified during this search.
